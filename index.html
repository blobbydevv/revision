How it works

Three steps, fifteen minutes a day

Cramming the night before loses to short, spaced sessions — every time it's been tested.

STEP 01

Pick a subject

26 CCEA subjects, each with its topic areas and a ready-made deck. No setup, no importing, nothing to buy.

STEP 02

Test yourself

Say the answer out loud before you flip. Rate it honestly — forgot, shaky, got it. That struggle is the bit that works.

STEP 03

Let it schedule you

Cards you forget return tomorrow. Cards you know drift out to 2, 4, 8 and 16 days. You just show up daily.

What's inside

Five ways to revise, one place

Spaced flashcards

A five-box Leitner system does the scheduling. Forgotten cards come straight back; mastered ones get out of your way.

Quick-fire quizzes

Eight multiple-choice questions per round. Anything you get wrong is pushed back to box one automatically.

Blurting

Seven minutes, no notes, write everything you remember. Then tick off what you got and send the gaps to your deck.

Focus timer

25/5, 50/10 or 15/3 blocks with a longer break every fourth one. One method per block, phone in another room.

Methods, ranked

Fourteen revision techniques rated by the strength of the evidence behind them — so you stop wasting nights on highlighting.

Mixed review

Due cards from every subject, shuffled. Interleaving feels harder than blocking — which is exactly why it sticks.

How the boxes work

Every card sits in a box from 1 to 5. Got it moves it up and pushes it further away — 1, 2, 4, 8, then 16 days. Shaky keeps it where it is for tomorrow. Forgot sends it back to box 1 and reshuffles it into this session.

Box 5 counts as mastered.

Focus timer

Work in short blocks with your phone in another room, then take a real break. After four blocks, take a longer one. Use each block for one active method — flashcards, a past paper question, or a blurt.
