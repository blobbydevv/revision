Mixed review

Due cards from every subject, shuffled together. Mixing subjects (interleaving) feels harder, and that is exactly why it sticks.

How the boxes work

Each card lives in box 1–5. Got it moves it up a box and pushes it further away (1, 2, 4, 8, then 16 days). Shaky keeps it where it is for tomorrow. Forgot sends it back to box 1. Box 5 counts as mastered.

Keys: Space flip, 1 forgot, 2 shaky, 3 got it.

Focus timer

Work in short, single-task blocks with your phone in another room, then take a real break. After four blocks, take a longer one. Use each block for one active method: flashcards, a past paper question, or a blurt.
